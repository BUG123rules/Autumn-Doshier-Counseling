Out-of-network & superbills

If I am not in-network with your plan, you may still be able to use your out-of-network benefits. I can provide a superbill — a detailed receipt you submit directly to your insurer for potential reimbursement.

What is a superbill?

A superbill is an itemized statement with the billing codes your insurance company needs to process a reimbursement claim. I provide these upon request after each session or on a monthly basis.

How do I check my out-of-network benefits?

Call the member services number on the back of your insurance card and ask about your out-of-network mental health benefits, your deductible, and your reimbursement rate for outpatient therapy.

Common questions

When is payment due?

Payment is due at the time of service. I accept credit and debit cards, HSA/FSA cards, and can discuss other arrangements as needed.

What is the cancellation policy?

I ask for at least 24 hours notice for cancellations. Late cancellations or no-shows may be charged a fee. We can discuss this in more detail during the initial consultation.

Do you offer telehealth sessions?

Yes. Telehealth sessions are available and covered by most of the insurance plans I accept. Please confirm telehealth coverage with your insurer before scheduling.

How do I verify my insurance benefits?

The easiest way is to call your insurance provider directly. When you reach out to schedule, I can also help clarify what information to ask for.

Questions about cost or coverage?

Don’t let uncertainty about fees be a barrier to reaching out. I’m happy to answer questions before you schedule, and a free 15-minute consultation is always available to make sure the fit feels right.
