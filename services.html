What I offer

Therapy shaped around you.

I work with adolescents and adults aged 14 and older, offering individual and couples counseling in a private practice setting in Southern Oregon. Sessions are designed to meet you where you are.

Counseling formats

Whether you are navigating something specific or seeking deeper understanding of yourself, I offer two formats tailored to different needs and goals.

Individual

Individual counseling

One-on-one therapy for adolescents and adults. We work at your pace, with space to explore what matters most to you — whether that is processing the past, managing day-to-day challenges, or building toward lasting change.

Couples

Couples counseling

Relational therapy for couples navigating communication, conflict, transitions, or deeper disconnection. Sessions focus on building understanding and working toward a stronger, more secure connection.

Free 15-minute consultation. Before scheduling an initial appointment, I offer a complimentary phone consultation to make sure the fit feels right for you.

Therapeutic approaches

My approach is eclectic and integrative — drawing from multiple methods to support the whole person, always rooted in your individual goals and needs.

Trauma-informed care

Attachment-focused work, EMDR, Brainspotting, and somatic resourcing techniques for acute and longer-term trauma recovery.

Humanistic and relational

Person-centered therapy grounded in respect, interpersonal neurobiology, and the belief that clients hold deep wisdom about their own healing.

Mind-body integration

Polyvagal-informed support and somatic interventions that connect lived experience in the body to emotional and psychological wellbeing.

Values-based and expressive

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Expressive Arts Therapy, and HAES/Intuitive Eating-informed support for identity, meaning, and growth.
